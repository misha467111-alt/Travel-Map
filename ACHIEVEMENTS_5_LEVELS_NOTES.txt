Travel Map — Achievements + 5 Levels

Added:
- 35 achievements across locations, check-ins, friends, photos, distance, comments and XP levels.
- 5 total XP levels:
  1. Новачок — 0 XP
  2. Дослідник — 100 XP
  3. Мандрівник — 300 XP
  4. Турист — 600 XP
  5. Першовідкривач — 1000 XP
- Each newly unlocked level grants +1 invite exactly once on the server.
- Achievement progress is calculated by Supabase RPC.
- Achievement screen shows total completion progress and individual progress.

Run in Supabase SQL Editor:
